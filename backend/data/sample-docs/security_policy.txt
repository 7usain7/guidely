# Corporate Security & Data Protection Policy

## 1. Password & Authentication Standards
- Passwords must be at least 16 characters long and stored exclusively within the company password manager (1Password).
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) via hardware security key (YubiKey) or authenticator app is mandatory across all internal tools. SMS-based MFA is strictly prohibited.

## 2. Data Classification & Handling
- **Public:** Marketing materials, published blog posts.
- **Internal:** General internal communications, roadmap docs.
- **Confidential:** Customer PII, source code, financial reports.
- **Restricted:** Credentials, production database keys, encryption secret keys.

Confidential and Restricted data must never be pasted into unapproved third-party AI tools or unencrypted external drives.

## 3. Laptop & Endpoint Security
Company-issued laptops must have Full Disk Encryption (FileVault / BitLocker) enabled at all times. Laptops automatically lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@guidely.com within 1 hour for remote wipe.
