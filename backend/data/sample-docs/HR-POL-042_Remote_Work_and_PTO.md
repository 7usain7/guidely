# Corporate Policy: Remote Work, Travel, and Paid Time Off (PTO)

**Document ID:** HR-POL-042  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Owner:** Human Resources / People Ops  
**Applies To:** All Full-Time and Part-Time Global Employees

---

## 1. Remote Work & Hybrid Schedule Policies

### 1.1 Work Location Classifications

Employees are assigned one of three work models upon hire or contract modification:

- **In-Office (Tier 1):** Assigned to a local corporate office. Required on-site 4 to 5 days per week.
- **Hybrid (Tier 2):** Required on-site a minimum of 2 designated core days per week (typically Tuesdays and Thursdays).
- **Fully Remote (Tier 3):** Authorized to work permanently from an approved remote location (home office or co-working space).

### 1.2 Remote Work Stipend & Equipment

- **Initial Setup Allowance:** Fully Remote (Tier 3) employees receive a one-time workspace stipend of **$750 USD** within their first 30 days of employment to purchase ergonomic office furniture, secondary monitors, or peripheral equipment.
- **Monthly Internet Allowance:** Hybrid and Remote employees receive a tax-free stipend of **$60 USD/month** toward home broadband internet.
- **Equipment Provisioning:** IT will issue a standard corporate laptop, headset, and security key. Personal computers are **strictly prohibited** from accessing internal network resources without explicit MDM (Mobile Device Management) approval.

---

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Entitlements

### 2.1 Accrual Schedule for Full-Time Staff

| Tenure Bracket  | Annual PTO Accrual     | Maximum Carryover (Dec 31) | Max Accrual Cap |
| :-------------- | :--------------------- | :------------------------- | :-------------- |
| **0 - 2 Years** | 15 Days (10.0 hrs/mo)  | 5 Days                     | 20 Days         |
| **3 - 5 Years** | 20 Days (13.33 hrs/mo) | 8 Days                     | 25 Days         |
| **6+ Years**    | 25 Days (16.66 hrs/mo) | 10 Days                    | 30 Days         |

_Note: Once an employee reaches their Maximum Accrual Cap, further PTO accrual pauses until existing balance is used._

### 2.2 Sick Leave and Mental Health Days

- Employees receive **8 paid Sick Days** per calendar year, granted upfront on January 1st.
- Sick leave does not roll over from year to year and is not paid out upon separation.
- Consecutive sick leave exceeding **3 business days** requires a doctor's note submitted directly to the HR Portal.

---

## 3. PTO Request Procedure

1. Submit requests through the **Workday HR Portal** at least **14 calendar days** prior to the requested start date for planned absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
2. Manager approval must be documented within 3 business days of submission.
3. Blackout dates apply during annual core platform migration windows (Q3 Sprint 4 and Q4 Deployment Lock out).
