# Internal IT & Support FAQ

## Q: How do I reset my SSO / Okta password?
A: Navigate to auth.guidely.internal and click "Forgot Password". A reset link will be sent to your backup email address or phone via SMS. If locked out completely, contact the IT Helpdesk on Slack channel #it-helpdesk or call extension 4357.

## Q: How do I request access to GitHub repositories or AWS accounts?
A: All access requests are managed via AccessPortal (access.guidely.internal). Submit a request specifying the repository or AWS IAM role needed along with approval from your Engineering Lead. Access is granted within 2 business hours.

## Q: What should I do if I suspect a phishing email or security incident?
A: Do not click any links or download attachments. Use the "Report Phishing" button in Google Workspace / Outlook, or forward the suspicious message directly to security-alerts@guidely.com immediately.

## Q: How do I set up VPN for remote internal tool access?
A: Download the WireGuard client from internal IT portal. Download your personalized client config file from vpn.guidely.internal using Okta 2FA, import it into WireGuard, and connect to the Primary-East endpoint.
