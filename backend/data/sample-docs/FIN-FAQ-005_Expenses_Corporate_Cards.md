# Finance FAQ: Employee Expenses, Procurement, and Brex Cards

**Document ID:** FIN-FAQ-005  
**Maintainer:** Global Accounts Payable & Accounting  
**Target Audience:** All Staff and Purchasing Managers

---

## 1. Corporate Credit Card Usage

**Question:** What is the company policy on corporate credit card usage and personal expenses?  
**Answer:** Corporate cards (issued via Brex) are provided to managers and employees with recurring purchasing needs (such as software subscriptions, digital advertising, or frequent travel). Personal expenses of any kind are **strictly forbidden** on corporate cards. Using a corporate card for personal purchases will result in automatic payroll deduction and potential disciplinary review.

---

## 2. Travel Expenses and Per Diem Limits

**Question:** What are the daily spending limits for Meals & Entertainment during business travel?  
**Answer:** When traveling for company business, per diem spending limits are enforced according to the location and meal type detailed below:

| Expense Category                        | Domestic Travel (US/Canada)      | International Travel        |
| :-------------------------------------- | :------------------------------- | :-------------------------- |
| **Breakfast**                           | $25 USD                          | $35 USD                     |
| **Lunch**                               | $30 USD                          | $45 USD                     |
| **Dinner**                              | $65 USD                          | $85 USD                     |
| **Client Entertainment (Per Head Cap)** | $120 USD (Manager Approval Reqd) | $150 USD (VP Approval Reqd) |

_Note: Alcohol is reimbursed ONLY if purchased during a client dinner and approved by a Manager._

## 3. Submission Deadlines & Receipt Requirements

**Question:** What is the deadline and receipt requirement for submitting expense reports?  
**Answer:**

- **Submission Deadline:** Expense reports must be submitted through **Ramp / Concur** by the **25th day of the month** following the month in which the expense was incurred.
- **Receipt Threshold:** Itemized receipts are **mandatory for any expense exceeding $25.00 USD**. Credit card slips showing only the total amount are not acceptable proof of itemized purchase.

---

## 4. Software Procurement & SaaS Approvals

**Question:** How do I request approval for new SaaS tools or software licenses?  
**Answer:** Any software purchase regardless of price must undergo security review prior to payment:

1. Open a ticket in **Jira Service Desk** under `Procurement > Software Request`.
2. Attach vendor details: vendor name, SOC 2 Type II compliance report, data privacy impact assessment, and annual cost.
3. Obtain necessary financial authorization: Purchases over **$5,000/year** require explicit sign-off from both the Finance Controller and the CISO.
