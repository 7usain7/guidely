# Guidely Employee Handbook

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Policy
Guidely provides flexible Paid Time Off for all full-time team members. We recommend employees take a minimum of 20 days per calendar year. Parental leave offers 16 weeks of 100% paid leave for primary caregivers and 8 weeks for secondary caregivers.

## 2. Health & Wellness Benefits
Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance begins on day one of employment. Guidely covers 90% of employee premiums and 75% of dependent premiums. Additionally, employees receive a $100 monthly wellness stipend usable for gym memberships, therapy apps, or sports gear.

## 3. Learning & Development Budget
Every employee has an annual L&D budget of $1,500 for attending industry conferences, purchasing technical books, or taking online courses relevant to their career trajectory.

## 4. Code of Conduct & Workplace Values
Guidely is committed to an inclusive, respectful, and psychological safe workplace. Discrimination, harassment, or retaliation of any kind will result in immediate disciplinary action up to termination.
