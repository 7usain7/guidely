# Remote Work & Expense Policy

## 1. Remote Work Eligibility & Guidelines
All full-time employees are eligible for hybrid or full-time remote work upon manager approval. Remote employees must maintain core working hours between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM in their local timezone.

## 2. Equipment Stipend
New remote hires are eligible for a standard home-office equipment stipend of up to $1,000 USD. This covers monitors, ergonomic chairs, keyboards, and desk accessories. Expense claims must be submitted via ExpensePort within 30 days of purchase with itemized receipts attached.

## 3. Internet & Connectivity Reimbursement
Guidely provides a monthly connectivity allowance of $70 USD to cover high-speed home internet costs for remote staff. Submissions are processed automatically on the 1st of each month.

## 4. Travel & Onsite Meetings
When traveling for company offsites or customer meetings, employees are permitted to book economy air travel and standard business hotels ($250/night maximum cap). Daily meal allowances are capped at $75 per day.
